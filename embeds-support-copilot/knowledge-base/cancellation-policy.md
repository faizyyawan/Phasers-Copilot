# Cancellation Policy

- Document ID: `cancellation-policy-v1`
- Version: `1.0`
- Category: `cancellations`

> Practice assumptions created for this learning project. These are not final business policies or legal terms.

## Purpose

Explain cancellation timing assumptions and when support should escalate.

## Policy or Procedure

- More than 24 hours before the slot may qualify for a full advance refund.
- Between 6 and 24 hours before the slot may qualify for a partial refund.
- Less than 6 hours before the slot is generally non-refundable.
- Court cancellation may qualify for a full refund or rescheduling.
- The chatbot must not guarantee eligibility.
- Disputed cases must be escalated.

## Examples

- A customer cancelling 30 hours before the slot may be eligible for full advance refund review.
- A customer cancelling 10 hours before the slot may be eligible for partial refund review.
- A customer cancelling 2 hours before the slot should be told it is generally non-refundable, with escalation if there is a dispute.

## Information Support Should Request

- Booking ID
- Reason for cancellation
- Slot date and time
- Request time
- Evidence for exceptional circumstances

## Escalation Conditions

- Customer disputes refund eligibility.
- Court owner cancelled the slot.
- Weather or venue safety issue is reported.
- Timing data is unclear or contradictory.
