# Refund Policy

- Document ID: `refund-policy-v1`
- Version: `1.0`
- Category: `refunds`

> Practice assumptions created for this learning project. These are not final business policies or legal terms.

## Purpose

Explain refund eligibility, required evidence, processing stages, and escalation conditions.

## Policy or Procedure

## Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on cancellation timing, payment verification, court cancellation, duplicate payment, or support review. The chatbot must not guarantee a refund.

## Required Evidence

Support may request booking ID, payment reference, receipt screenshot, cancellation reason, and timing details.

## Processing Stages

Typical stages are `review_requested`, `refund_pending`, and `refunded`. A delayed refund should be checked with the payment status tool.

## Duplicate Payments

Duplicate payments should be escalated for review with both payment references.

## Failed Refunds

Failed refunds require support review and may need corrected payment details.

## Court Cancellations

If the court cancels, the customer may qualify for full refund or rescheduling.

## Customer Cancellations

Customer cancellation follows the timing assumptions in [Cancellation Policy](cancellation-policy.md).

## Delayed Refunds

Support should check current payment/refund status before giving a timeline.

## Examples

- A confirmed duplicate payment should be escalated with both payment references.
- A refund marked `refund_pending` should not be described as completed.

## Information Support Should Request

- Booking ID
- Payment ID or reference
- Amount
- Cancellation time
- Reason and evidence

## Escalation Conditions

- Duplicate payment
- Failed refund
- Disputed cancellation timing
- Court cancellation
- Refund delay after status shows pending
