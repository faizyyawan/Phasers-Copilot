# Court Owner Guide

- Document ID: `court-owner-guide-v1`
- Version: `1.0`
- Category: `court_owners`

> Practice assumptions created for this learning project. These are not final business policies or legal terms.

## Purpose

Explain support guidance for court owners.

## Policy or Procedure

Court owners are responsible for keeping availability accurate. A slot should not be marked available if the court is closed, under maintenance, or reserved outside the platform.

Future structured tools may read availability. The first chatbot version must not change availability automatically.

Court owners should notify support as early as possible if a confirmed booking cannot be honored. Court cancellation may qualify the customer for full refund or rescheduling.

## Examples

- A court owner blocks a slot for maintenance before customers book it.
- A court owner reports floodlight failure after bookings are confirmed; support escalates affected bookings.

## Information Support Should Request

- Court ID or court name
- Owner account identity
- Date and time slots affected
- Reason for availability change
- Whether customers are already booked

## Escalation Conditions

- Confirmed booking cannot be honored.
- Owner cannot update availability.
- Multiple bookings affected by court issue.
- Owner requests automatic cancellation or refund.
