# Customer Account Guide

- Document ID: `customer-account-guide-v1`
- Version: `1.0`
- Category: `accounts`

> Practice assumptions created for this learning project. These are not final business policies or legal terms.

## Purpose

Explain customer account support topics for the fictional platform.

## Policy or Procedure

Customers should keep their phone number and email updated because booking, payment, and cancellation notifications may depend on them. Support should verify identity before discussing booking-specific information.

Customers can ask general policy questions without authentication, but booking-specific status requires authorization and a structured tool.

## Examples

- "How do I book?" can be answered from documents.
- "What is my booking status?" requires identity and booking lookup.
- "Change my phone number" should be handled by account workflow, not by the chatbot automatically.

## Information Support Should Request

- Account phone or email
- Booking ID if relevant
- Description of account issue

## Escalation Conditions

- User cannot access account.
- Phone number is wrong on an active booking.
- User requests another customer's data.
- Suspicious account ownership claim.
