# General FAQ

- Document ID: `general-faq-v1`
- Version: `1.0`
- Category: `faq`

> Practice assumptions created for this learning project. These are not final business policies or legal terms.

## Purpose

Answer common general questions for the fictional support copilot.

## Policy or Procedure

## How do I book a court?

Select a court, date, and time slot. Submit the 30% advance within 30 minutes. Booking is confirmed after payment verification.

## Why is my booking pending?

It may be waiting for payment or payment verification. Current status requires the booking status tool.

## Can I cancel?

Cancellation may be possible, but refund eligibility depends on timing and review.

## When will my refund arrive?

Refund status requires the payment status tool. The chatbot must not guarantee completion timing.

## Why did I not receive WhatsApp?

Possible reasons include invalid phone number, opt-out, provider failure, or delivery delay. Current delivery history requires the notification tool.

## Examples

- General payment percentage questions are RAG questions.
- Specific booking status questions are tool questions.

## Information Support Should Request

- Booking ID for booking-specific questions
- Payment reference for payment questions
- Phone confirmation for notification questions

## Escalation Conditions

- User asks for guaranteed refund.
- User requests another customer's data.
- Documents do not answer the question.
- Current tool data conflicts with user evidence.
